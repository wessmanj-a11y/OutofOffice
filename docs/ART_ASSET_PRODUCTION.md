# OUT OF OFFICE Custom Asset Production Brief

## Target visual style
The game art should match the approved mockup: premium comic-book office satire with bold ink lines, exaggerated expressions, dramatic shadows, gritty panel texture, readable silhouettes, and modern collectible-card polish.

## Critical style rules
- Comic book meets The Oatmeal energy, but professionally illustrated.
- Expressive, exaggerated, funny, readable.
- Not childish, not crude, not anime, not generic corporate clip art.
- Use thick ink outlines, halftone texture, angular panels, punchy colors, and dramatic office lighting.
- Every card should read instantly at mobile size.

## Image generation prompt template
Create a polished comic-book illustration for a satirical corporate card game called OUT OF OFFICE. Style: bold ink outlines, expressive exaggerated faces, professional graphic-novel rendering, The Oatmeal-inspired humor, modern collectible card game polish, gritty office lighting, halftone texture, dramatic composition, readable silhouette, funny but not childish, premium indie strategy game art. Aspect ratio 4:3 for card art. No text in image unless specifically requested.

## Individual card prompts

### Reply All Disaster
A panicked office worker accidentally hitting send as hundreds of paper-envelope emails explode around them like shrapnel. Corporate cubicle chaos, terrified expression, red danger lighting, comic action lines.

### Budget Cuts
A smug executive with huge scissors cutting desks, laptops, and office chairs in half while exhausted workers panic. Darkly funny, absurd corporate cruelty, red/orange palette.

### Mandatory Overtime
Zombie-like office workers under harsh fluorescent lights, coffee IV drips, moon outside window, printer smoking in background. Funny workplace horror.

### Mental Health Day
A relieved employee wrapped in a blanket on a couch, phone face-down, Slack notifications burning harmlessly in the distance, birds visible through a window.

### Vacation Approved
An employee holding a glowing approval email like a sacred artifact while beach light pours through a cubicle wall crack.

### Work From Home
A tired but peaceful worker at a laptop at home, pajama pants, pet nearby, chaotic office ghosts outside the window trying to get in.

### Promotion Opportunity
A worker climbing a dangerous corporate ladder into storm clouds, briefcase in hand, glowing office tower above.

### Executive Visibility
A worker frozen under a giant spotlight from a boardroom of shadowy executives, terrified but smiling professionally.

### LinkedIn Thought Leader
A polished business influencer ascending in a ridiculous aura of hashtags, motivational buzzwords, and self-congratulation.

### Quiet Quit Ritual
A calm employee in a dark hoodie slowly fading into myth while notification bubbles burn around them, serene and mysterious.
